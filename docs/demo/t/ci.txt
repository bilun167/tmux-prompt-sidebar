> why do macOS CI runs miss the cache

⏺ The cache key hashes package-lock.json from
  the repo root, but the lockfile is in web/.

⏺ Edit(.github/workflows/ci.yml)
- key: deps-${{ hashFiles('package-lock.json') }}
+ key: deps-${{ hashFiles('web/package-lock.json') }}
